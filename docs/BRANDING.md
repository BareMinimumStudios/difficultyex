The identity retains the old DX initials, ivory file-card shape and green, amber and red difficulty accents. It follows the Remnant studio family with restrained typography and a dark background. The banner uses only the words **Difficulty EX**.

Keep the aspect ratio and safe margins. Do not stretch the logo or place extra text over the banner. Artwork is covered by the asset terms in [BML v1.0](../LICENSE).

The refreshed logo and banner were created with the built-in image generator using the supplied old logo and Remnant artwork as references. Prompts: refine the DX file-card monogram with clean flat geometry and three difficulty bars; create a matching wide dark banner with the exact words "Difficulty EX", the icon at left and a restrained tricolor accent. No external photography was used.
