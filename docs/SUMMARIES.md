## Short description

Configurable MMORPG-style mob levels powered by PlayerEx, with area rules, scaled stats and optional nameplates.

## Expanded description

Shape your world's difficulty with PlayerEx-driven mob levels. Configure dimension, biome, structure and entity rules, scale health, armor, damage and XP, and choose the nameplate details you want to see. The Minecraft 1.21.1 Fabric and NeoForge port is in development.
